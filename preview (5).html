Dear My Favourite Person,

Three years. Think about that. Three years of conversations, laughter, memories, little arguments, forgiveness, and countless moments that became special simply because you were a part of them.

Sometimes I wish the world were a little smaller. I wish I could just appear beside you, hold your hand, look into your eyes and tell you everything that words have never been able to explain.

I want to spend my life with you. Not only the beautiful days, but also the ordinary ones. I want the mornings, the evenings, the silly conversations, the adventures, the peaceful moments and everything in between.

"I don't just want you in my favourite memories. I want you in all the memories I haven't made yet."

I love you so much. More than a message can explain, more than a website can display, and more than these paragraphs could ever contain.

Until The Day I Reach You

🫂

"One day, I won't have to send you a hug. I'll finally be there to give you one."

Until that day, let the distance do whatever it wants. Let the kilometres exist. Let the clocks keep moving. I will keep looking forward to the moment when we finally get to share the same place.

I imagine us walking together, exploring new places, drinking tea, laughing about the smallest things and making memories that no screen could ever capture.

"I'm not dreaming of a perfect world. I'm dreaming of an ordinary world where you're beside me."

A Promise From My Heart

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise that I will try to understand, communicate, respect your feelings and never take your presence for granted.

I want us to grow, support each other's dreams, celebrate happiness and stand beside each other through the different chapters of life.

"I don't want a temporary chapter. I hope we get to write a beautiful lifetime."
